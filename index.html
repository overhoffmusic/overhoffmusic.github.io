Bio

A passion for groove combined with an obsession for drive defines Amsterdam-based producer and DJ Overhoff. Drawing inspiration from a wide range of genres, his sound is built around forward movement, groove and drive, with a dynamic and propulsive approach firmly focused on the dancefloor.

After developing his craft at the Conservatorium van Amsterdam (AEMA), Overhoff began translating that foundation into his own direction within house music. Since then, he has released music on labels including Blanc and Psicodelica, while steadily expanding his own catalogue and further shaping his sound.

That momentum has also carried into his DJ career, with Overhoff becoming an increasingly familiar name across the Dutch club circuit. Playing regularly in Amsterdam and Rotterdam and appearing at some of the cities' leading clubs and venues, he continues to build his career both in the studio and behind the decks.
